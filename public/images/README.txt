Place local brand assets here (logo, favicon, team photos, etc.) and
reference them as `/images/filename.jpg`.

Currently the site pulls stock photography from images.unsplash.com
(already whitelisted in next.config.js) so it renders fully out of the
box. Swap any `src="https://images.unsplash.com/..."` in the
components/content files for `/images/your-file.jpg` once you have
real photography.
